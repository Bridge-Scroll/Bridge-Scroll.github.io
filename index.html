Scroll Bridge.

The Scroll Bridge brings ETH, USDC and tokens onto Scroll, a bytecode-equivalent zkEVM that runs Ethereum contracts unchanged and proves every batch with zero-knowledge validity proofs. You see the amount received and the fee before you sign, pay gas in ETH, and keep your own keys throughout.

How the estimate is calculated

Fee = 0.25 units + 0.3% of the amount, rounded to two decimal places and capped at the amount sent. The remaining amount uses the same units, at a 1:1 conversion. Time starts at 2 minutes, adds 1 minute per 2,500 units or part thereof, and is capped at 12 minutes. The displayed range adds 2 minutes to that estimate.

What the Scroll Bridge actually does

The Scroll Bridge locks your deposit in an Ethereum contract and credits the same asset on Scroll, where it behaves exactly as on mainnet because the zkEVM is bytecode-equivalent. The same contracts, tooling and addresses you already use just work, so a bridged token needs no special wrapper.

Why validity proofs make the Scroll Bridge trust-minimized

Every batch of Scroll transactions is proven to Ethereum with a zero-knowledge validity proof, so the base chain mathematically verifies state rather than trusting an operator. For you that means a withdrawal does not rely on a watchtower or a long fraud window — it clears once the proof is finalized on Ethereum.

Timing: deposits, withdrawals and Scroll Bridge finality

Deposits credit on Scroll within minutes of your Ethereum transaction confirming. Withdrawals back to Ethereum wait for the next validity proof to be posted and finalized, which is why the exit side takes longer than the entry side. The interface shows the current estimate before you commit.

What a Scroll Bridge transfer costs

You pay Ethereum gas to deposit; once on Scroll, transactions cost a small fraction of mainnet, split between L2 execution and the data the rollup posts to Ethereum. The quote shows the net amount received, so size your transfer against the fee rather than bridging dust.

Using Scroll after you bridge

On Scroll your assets plug straight into EVM apps — DEXs, lending and liquidity — with the familiar MetaMask flow and Ethereum addresses. Keep a little ETH on Scroll for gas, and when you want native ETH back on mainnet, withdraw through the same canonical route.
Is Scroll really bytecode-equivalent?
Yes. Scroll runs the same EVM bytecode as Ethereum, so existing contracts and tools work without changes, and bridged tokens keep their normal behavior.
Why does a Scroll withdrawal take longer than a deposit?
Deposits credit after Ethereum confirmations, but withdrawals wait for a validity proof to be finalized on Ethereum before funds are released.
Do I need ETH on Scroll?
Yes, a small amount of ETH covers gas on Scroll once your assets arrive.
Do I need an account to use the Scroll Bridge?
No. You connect a self-custody wallet and bridge on-chain, with no sign-up and no custody.
What if my deposit transaction fails?
It reverts and you keep your assets; only the Ethereum gas for the attempt is spent.
